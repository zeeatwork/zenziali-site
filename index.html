About Me

How I Approach Quality

I approach quality as an ongoing process of understanding risk, asking better questions, and giving teams the information they need to make good decisions. Testing is part of that work, but so are the systems, conversations, and feedback loops that help prevent problems in the first place.

Understand the Risk

I look beyond whether a feature works as expected to understand what could fail, who would be affected, and what matters most to test.

Build Quality Into the Process

I look for ways to improve the systems around testing, from clearer requirements and traceability to better bug reporting, release checks, and feedback loops.

Keep the User in the Conversation

I consider how people actually use a product, including their environment, constraints, and expectations, so quality decisions reflect more than whether the software technically works.

About Me

I came to Quality Engineering through a career built around solving problems, improving processes, and making complicated work easier to understand. Over the past five years, I've brought that perspective to software testing, test automation, quality strategy, and, more recently, AI evaluation.

I'm especially interested in the space where technical skill, curiosity, and quality thinking meet. Whether I'm investigating a failure, building automation, or watching how an AI model handles a question it wasn't built for, I want to understand not only whether something works, but why, for whom, and what could happen next.
