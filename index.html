About Me

How I Approach Quality

On every team, I start with the same three habits. They work whether I'm the only tester or one of many.

Understand the Risk

Before I test, I ask what could fail, who it would affect, and what matters most to check first.

Build Quality Into the Process

I improve the systems around testing: clearer requirements, tests that trace back to their source, bug reports engineers can act on, and release checks that catch problems early.

Keep the User in the Conversation

I test the way people actually use the product, in their environment and with their constraints, so "it works" also means "it works for them."

About Me

I came to Quality Engineering through a career built around solving problems, improving processes, and making complicated work easier to understand. Over the past five years, I've brought that perspective to software testing, test automation, quality strategy, and, more recently, AI evaluation.

I'm especially interested in the space where technical skill, curiosity, and quality thinking meet. Whether I'm investigating a failure, building automation, or watching how an AI model handles a question it wasn't built for, I want to understand not only whether something works, but why, for whom, and what could happen next.
